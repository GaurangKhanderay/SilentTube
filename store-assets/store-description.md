## Full Description
**Take back control of your YouTube experience with SilentTube.**

SilentTube is a lightweight, privacy-first focus mode extension that lets you hide distracting elements on YouTube so you can concentrate on what you actually want to watch.

**✨ Features: Toggle exactly what you want to hide:**
*   🩳 **Shorts:** Remove the Shorts shelf and sidebar links.
*   📺 **Recommendations:** Hide the right-side recommended videos sidebar.
*   🏠 **Home Feed:** Empty the homepage grid to prevent mindless browsing.
*   🎬 **End Cards:** Remove clickable video thumbnails at the end of videos.
*   ▶️ **Autoplay:** Disable the "up next" autoplay button.
*   💬 **Comments:** Hide the entire comments section.
*   📈 **Trending:** Remove the Trending tab.

**🔒 Privacy First. Guaranteed.**
SilentTube is built with an absolute commitment to privacy.
*   **Zero Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, browsing history, or YouTube activity.
*   **100% Local:** All your toggle preferences are stored locally on your device (or synced securely via your Google account if Chrome Sync is enabled).
*   **No Network Calls:** The extension is architecturally incapable of phoning home. It makes zero network requests.
*   **No Trackers:** No analytics, no ads, no affiliate links.

**⚡ Lightning Fast:**
SilentTube uses less than 20KB of code and adds zero noticeable overhead to your browsing experience. It simply injects tiny CSS rules to hide elements before they even render.

Get focused. Install SilentTube today.
